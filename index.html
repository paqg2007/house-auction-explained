The Big Idea

The whole project in 3 steps

Every team makes a house, sells it at auction, and buys other teams’ houses.

1

Create

Draw a house and make an advertisement for it.

2

Sell

Pitch your house in 2 minutes. The class bids on it.

3

Buy

Spend fake money on the houses you like best.

Step 1 · Create

Work in pairs. Draw it, don’t build it.

Teams of 2

You and your partner are one team. You share the work and the grade.

Draw your house

Draw it in your own style. Make it a house people will want to buy.

Make an advertisement

Your ad has to sell the house. You do not build anything. Drawing + ad is the whole job.

Step 2 · Auction Day

Pitch fast. Then the bidding starts.

Your pitch: 2 minutes

Show your drawing and your ad. Tell the class why your house is worth buying.

Mr. Quintero is the auctioneer

He runs the sale. Teams raise their bids, the price goes up, and the highest bid wins the house.

2 minutes per team. That’s it.
Step 3 · Your Money

Every team gets…

$0

Fake money. Spend it all on houses, you can’t keep the cash.

Buy up to  2 houses At the end, you  keep 1 You can  sell 1 back

Each part counts the same. A weak part pulls your grade down, a strong part pulls it up.

Part 1 of 3 60 to 100

Quality: my grade for YOUR work

This part is about the house your team created. Nothing to do with money.

Your drawing

Care, detail and effort in the house you drew.

Your advertisement

Does your ad make people want the house? Is it clear and convincing?

Better work = higher Part 1. This grade also hides inside your house as its secret grade. Remember that for Part 3.

Part 2 of 3 59 to 100

Selling Price: how much YOUR house sells for

All the sale prices are lined up, highest to lowest. Your place in that line is your grade.

Highest price
100
In the middle
60–100
Lowest price that still sells
60
Your house does not sell
59

A great pitch and a great ad push your price up. No sale is the only way to get a 59.

Part 3 of 3 59 to 100

Secret Grade: the house you KEEP grades YOU

Every house hides a secret grade: its Part 1 quality grade. Nobody knows it while bidding.

You find out only when you buy the house. The secret grade of the house you keep at the end becomes your Part 3.

Buy no house at all? Your Part 3 is 59.

Before We Start

The one thing to remember

Buy the house you think is GOOD, not just the cheap one.

The house you keep hands you its secret grade. A beautiful, well-made house can pay you back in Part 3, even if it cost you more fake money.
